FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does TalentRadar know which jobs match my experience?

When you upload your CV, our engine extracts your verified skills, experience level, and tech stack. Each scraped job is evaluated across key tech keywords, seniority markers, and architectural responsibilities to provide a 0–100% objective fit score.

Which regions and job boards are tracked?

We actively track the Gulf tech market (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Jeddah, Doha, Kuwait, Bahrain) as well as the Pakistan technology ecosystem (Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi) and Global Remote employers willing to hire and sponsor international talent.

Will the CV templates pass Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)?

Yes. Our templates strictly avoid tables, graphics, text boxes, and multi-column designs that cause Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever to fail. They adhere to clean typography, standard headers, and chronological work history.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, there are no contracts or commitments. You can cancel with a single click inside your billing settings at any time.
